Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what is needed to run Spendly:

  • Account details such as your name and email address.
  • Expense records you enter: amount, category, date, and description.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide the service: showing your expenses, category breakdowns, and summaries. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a database on our servers. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no system is completely secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when your browser requests them. We do not share your expense data with any third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want your data deleted, contact us at support@spendly.example.
